Sometimes, internal documentation isn’t enough.  
Sometimes, notes in Notion disappear.  
Sometimes, you want to write something that’s more than just a *how-to*.

And at that point… I realized:  
I needed my own space. A place to write, to store things, and to remind myself.

---

## ✍️ So, why do I write?

- To avoid repeating the same mistakes.
- To share things with teammates without explaining them over and over again.
- To help someone out there who’s Googling and happens to find something useful — even if it’s small.

---

## 🛠 What will I write about?

Mostly things like:
- Infrastructure (cloud, Linux, containers, monitoring)
- Automation (Terraform, Ansible, CI/CD, simple bash scripts)
- Small insights that come from real troubleshooting
- And sometimes… personal opinions about the DevOps world itself

---

## 🙏 Finally…

I don’t promise to write consistently.  
But I do promise that everything I write, I’ll try to make genuinely useful.

If something I write helps you, let me know.  
If something’s wrong — even better — let me know sooner.

Thanks for stopping by. ✌️
